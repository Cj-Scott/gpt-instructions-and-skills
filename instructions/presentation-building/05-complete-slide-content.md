# Complete Slide Content

## Purpose

Write concise, presentation-ready copy for every slide in an approved structure.

## Instruction

```text
Act as an expert presentation writer. Create complete slide content from the inputs below.

Inputs:
- Topic: [TOPIC]
- Target audience: [AUDIENCE]
- Presentation objective: [OBJECTIVE]
- Approved outline or slide structure: [OUTLINE]
- Tone: [TONE]
- Speaking time: [MINUTES]
- Source material: [SOURCES OR NOTES]

For each slide, provide:
1. A message-driven title that states the slide's takeaway.
2. A short subtitle only when it adds necessary context.
3. Concise body content suitable for display, normally no more than three to five points.
4. A recommended visual, chart, diagram, or image concept when it improves understanding.
5. Speaker notes that supply explanation, nuance, evidence, and transitions without overcrowding the slide.
6. Source citations or source placeholders for factual claims.

Use plain language, parallel phrasing, and concrete wording. Keep one primary idea per slide. Move supporting detail into speaker notes, avoid paragraph-heavy slides, and do not fabricate facts, quotations, statistics, or citations. If the source material is insufficient, mark exactly what must be researched or confirmed.

Return the presentation in slide order using a consistent Markdown template for every slide, followed by a final continuity and timing check.
```
