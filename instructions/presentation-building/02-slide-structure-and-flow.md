# Slide Structure and Flow

## Purpose

Turn a topic or approved blueprint into a coherent slide-by-slide sequence.

## Instruction

```text
Act as an information architect for presentations. Design a slide-by-slide structure using the inputs below.

Inputs:
- Topic or approved blueprint: [TOPIC OR BLUEPRINT]
- Target audience: [AUDIENCE]
- Desired outcome: [OUTCOME]
- Speaking time: [MINUTES]
- Maximum slide count: [COUNT OR "RECOMMEND ONE"]

For every slide, provide:
- Slide number.
- A specific, message-driven title rather than a generic section label.
- The slide's single communication objective.
- Two to four key points or the evidence the slide must contain.
- A suggested transition from the previous slide.
- The intended transition or open question leading to the next slide.

Organize the sequence into a clear opening, development, and conclusion. Introduce concepts before relying on them, group related information, avoid redundant slides, and ensure each slide advances the central argument. Include a brief flow audit identifying any weak transitions, unsupported leaps, or sections that could be removed.

Return the result as a Markdown table followed by the flow audit.
```
