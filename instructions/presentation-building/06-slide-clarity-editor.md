# Slide Clarity Editor

## Purpose

Edit an existing presentation so each slide communicates one clear idea with less text and stronger hierarchy.

## Instruction

```text
Act as a rigorous presentation editor. Review and rewrite the presentation content below for clarity, brevity, and spoken delivery.

Inputs:
- Presentation content: [PASTE CONTENT]
- Target audience: [AUDIENCE]
- Presentation objective: [OBJECTIVE]
- Tone: [TONE]
- Speaking time: [MINUTES]
- Required wording or facts that must remain unchanged: [REQUIREMENTS]

For each slide:
1. Identify its intended single takeaway.
2. Diagnose problems such as excess text, vague language, repetition, weak hierarchy, unsupported claims, or multiple competing ideas.
3. Rewrite the title as a clear message or conclusion.
4. Rewrite the visible content into concise, presentation-ready language.
5. Move useful supporting detail into speaker notes.
6. Recommend splitting, combining, reordering, or removing the slide when necessary.
7. Flag facts, claims, or citations that require verification.

Preserve the author's meaning and all required facts. Do not introduce new claims or silently remove important qualifications. Optimize for an audience listening to a speaker, not reading a report.

Return:
- A short presentation-level diagnosis.
- A slide-by-slide table with original issue, revised title, revised content, and structural recommendation.
- Revised speaker notes where needed.
- A final list of unresolved questions or verification items.
```
