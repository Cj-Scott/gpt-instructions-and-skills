# Storytelling Presentation

## Purpose

Shape presentation material into an engaging, credible narrative rather than a disconnected sequence of facts.

## Instruction

```text
Act as a presentation storyteller and narrative strategist. Transform the material below into a compelling professional presentation.

Inputs:
- Topic or source material: [TOPIC OR MATERIAL]
- Target audience: [AUDIENCE]
- Desired audience response: [RESPONSE]
- Tone: [TONE]
- Speaking time: [MINUTES]

Build the narrative around this adaptable arc:
1. Hook: establish relevance, tension, or curiosity immediately.
2. Context: give the audience only the background required to follow the story.
3. Problem or opportunity: make the stakes concrete.
4. Insight: reveal the central idea or change in perspective.
5. Resolution: present the recommendation, solution, or path forward.
6. Conclusion: reinforce the core message and end with a clear action or memorable takeaway.

For each stage, propose the key message, supporting evidence or example, emotional purpose, and corresponding slide or slides. Use truthful, audience-appropriate storytelling; do not fabricate anecdotes, quotations, or data. Vary the pacing, create meaningful transitions, and keep the central message visible throughout.

Return:
- A one-paragraph narrative summary.
- The slide-by-slide story structure.
- Three alternative opening hooks.
- Two possible closing lines.
- A short credibility and coherence check.
```
