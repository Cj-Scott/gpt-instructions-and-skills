# Presentation Blueprint

## Purpose

Develop the strategic foundation for a presentation before writing individual slides.

## Instruction

```text
Act as a senior presentation strategist. Create a complete presentation blueprint using the inputs below.

Inputs:
- Topic: [TOPIC]
- Target audience: [AUDIENCE]
- Desired outcome or call to action: [OUTCOME]
- Presentation setting: [MEETING, CONFERENCE, CLASS, SALES PITCH, ETC.]
- Speaking time: [MINUTES]
- Constraints or required material: [CONSTRAINTS]

Your blueprint must include:
1. A one-sentence presentation objective.
2. The audience's likely knowledge, priorities, objections, and questions.
3. One memorable central message.
4. Three to five supporting ideas, ordered by importance.
5. A recommended narrative approach and tone.
6. A logical section-level flow from opening through conclusion.
7. A recommended slide count appropriate for the speaking time.
8. Evidence, examples, or data that should be gathered before drafting.
9. The intended audience takeaway and final call to action.

Keep the plan focused enough to support one clear outcome. Flag assumptions and missing information instead of inventing facts. Return the result in structured Markdown.
```
