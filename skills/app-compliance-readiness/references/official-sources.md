# Official Source Routing

Use current primary sources. Record the retrieval date and applicability conditions for each material conclusion. If an official page conflicts with this reference, the current official page controls.

## United States regulators and law

- FTC business guidance and enforcement: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance
- FTC penalty-offense authority: https://www.ftc.gov/enforcement/penalty-offenses
- California Attorney General CCPA guidance: https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa
- California Privacy Protection Agency: https://cppa.ca.gov/
- U.S. Copyright Office DMCA overview: https://www.copyright.gov/dmca/
- Copyright Office Section 512 resources: https://www.copyright.gov/512/
- DMCA Designated Agent Directory FAQ: https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/faq.html

## Platform policy

- Apple App Privacy Details: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/
- Apple App Store Review Guidelines: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- Google Play Data Safety form: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469
- Google Play User Data policy: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311
- Google Play Developer Program Policies: https://play.google.com/about/developer-content-policy/

## Vendor documentation

Use the vendor's current documentation for each exact SDK and platform. Confirm version, enabled modules, automatic collection, configuration defaults, consent behavior, processor role, retention, deletion, identifiers, and custom-event contents.

## Evidence rules

- Prefer statutes, regulations, regulator guidance, official platform policy, and first-party vendor documentation.
- Use law-firm articles only to identify issues for verification; do not use them as final authority.
- Do not convert a maximum penalty into an automatic fine.
- Do not generalize one enforcement action into a universal disclosure rule.
- State when a source is guidance rather than binding law.
- Route unsettled interpretation, contractual enforceability, and jurisdiction-specific drafting to qualified counsel.
