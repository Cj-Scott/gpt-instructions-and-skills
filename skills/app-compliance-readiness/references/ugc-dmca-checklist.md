# UGC and DMCA Checklist

Use for apps that store user content, transmit it, cache it, or provide links or search results. This is a readiness checklist, not a safe-harbor determination.

## Product and jurisdiction scope

- Identify content types, visibility, hosting location, user locations, monetization, moderation, and recommendation behavior.
- Determine which Section 512 category or categories may be relevant; route eligibility interpretation to U.S. copyright counsel.
- Identify non-U.S. notice, platform, and intermediary-liability regimes separately.

## Section 512 operational evidence

- Current Copyright Office designated-agent registration and public matching contact information
- Renewal or resubmission tracking
- Complete notice intake, validation, logging, and response process
- Uploader notification and counter-notice process
- Restoration timing and escalation controls
- Adopted and reasonably implemented repeat-infringer policy
- Process for actual knowledge and red-flag escalation
- Handling of standard technical measures where applicable
- Trained owner, backup coverage, response targets, and audit trail

The current Copyright Office fee may be $6, but verify it from the official FAQ before stating it. Registration and a terms clause alone do not establish safe-harbor protection.

## Output

Return:

1. Product scope and assumptions
2. Operational evidence present
3. Missing or stale evidence
4. Immediate launch blockers
5. Draft process improvements
6. Questions for qualified counsel

Do not provide a guarantee against infringement claims or copy statutory damages into the report as an automatic exposure amount.
